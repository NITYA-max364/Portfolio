Projects

Some of the things I have worked on and continue to explore.

Food Leftover Management

A first-semester hackathon project focused on reducing food wastage by creating a practical solution for managing and redistributing leftover food. The project explored how technology can help connect surplus food with people who can benefit from it.

Tap-A-Toe

An ECE project designed to make digital interaction more accessible for people with physical disabilities. The concept enables users to control actions such as scrolling through reels using toe-based interaction, providing a hands-free and alternative way to operate a device.

Smart Mandi for Farmers

A second-semester project focused on helping farmers make better decisions when selling their crops. The idea was to help farmers compare available mandi prices and choose a suitable market where they could sell their produce at a better price, reducing the chances of missing out on fair market value.
